# SynIRgy — Research atlas

A working desktop/mobile mockup implemented in the existing Next.js site.

Palette: ink #142f62, cobalt #265bd8, ice #eaf2fc, paper #ffffff, slate #52647c, clay #dc7045 (diagram accent only).
Typography: Avenir Next / Trebuchet MS for display and body, with a generous title scale and quiet readable research copy. Local fallbacks avoid external font requests.
Layout: wide split hero, a concise event strip, an explanatory research loop, five topic rows, and an author dates / participation section. Supporting pages retain a narrower reading measure and share the generated banner treatment.

Desktop: [ title + purpose + action | generated data landscape ]
         [ date | location | format ]
         [ rationale | accessible simulation flow ]
         [ five research themes with symbolic diagrams ]
         [ dates table | participation ]
Mobile: same reading order, single column, illustration below title, wrapping flow.

Design review: use the workshop's methodological subject as the main visual; avoid generic AI brains, decorative charts implying results, promotional metrics, or repeated SaaS cards. Infographics are explanatory, not empirical. Keep all dates and uncertainty labels from the source. Preserve existing routes, submission details, organizer portraits, and archive.
